- **Team voice captions.** A new mode in the Live tab translates your team call live, with each speaker's name in their own colour, in the floating caption window and the OBS browser source. Built for mixed-language comms, such as Egyptian Arabic mixed with English and League terms.
- Pick the device the call plays on (for example "Discord (Elgato Virtual Audio)") and, optionally, your microphone so your own lines show with your name.
- **Learn each voice once:** add a teammate, let them talk for about 15 seconds, and their lines are named from then on. Click a name to rename it; captions already on screen and on OBS update too.
- About 1–2 s delay. Everything runs locally on your GPU.
- Each session saves a transcript with names and times, and appears in History.
