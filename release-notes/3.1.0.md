- **Translate video files from your PC.** In the Clip tab, click **Video file**, drag a video onto the window, or paste its path. Pick the section on the timeline and translate it like a VOD: subtitles, burned-in video, transcript and History all work the same way.
- Works with mp4, mkv, mov, webm, avi and most other formats. The sound is converted automatically, and video formats an mp4 can't hold are re-encoded.
