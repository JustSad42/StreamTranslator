- **Discord bot mode for Team voice.** A bot joins your team's voice channel and hears everyone separately, so every line is named after the right person automatically, even when people talk over each other. No voice learning needed. Paste the bot token, press Connect, pick the channel, and send the invite link to whoever manages the server.
- Everyone in the call is listed by their Discord name, with a dot that lights up while they talk. Click a name to change how it shows on stream; ↺ goes back to their Discord name. Renames are remembered.
- **Caption style for OBS.** Text size, background, top or bottom position, distance from the edge, width, alignment, lines on screen, how long lines stay, how speaker names show (colour, above the line, white or off), font, text outline and dimming older lines. There's a live preview, and changes show in OBS right away; old links keep working.
- **Better live translation.** Names of everyone in the call are given to Whisper so they're spelled right; an unclear bit of audio can no longer hold up captions for several seconds; repeated phrases are trimmed; coughs and clicks no longer turn into invented lines.
