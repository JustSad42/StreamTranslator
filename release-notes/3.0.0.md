StreamTranslator 3.0 is the same app rebuilt from the ground up in Rust. Everything works the way it did, with the same tabs and settings, and it keeps your history, glossary, models and GPU libraries.

- **Much smaller:** the installer is 100 MB (was 362 MB) and the app takes 378 MB on disk (was 1.1 GB).
- **Opens instantly:** the window shows in about half a second, even on the first start after booting (was up to 3.4 s).
- **Same translations:** Whisper and the translator give the same results as 2.3.1 on the same clips. Full clip jobs with voice isolation, speakers and burn-in are a little faster.
- **Notifications** now show as StreamTranslator with its icon.
- Works on NVIDIA GTX 10-series cards and newer, up to RTX 50. GTX 900-series cards are no longer supported for the GPU.

If anything isn't right, **Go back to the old version** in this window or in Settings reinstalls 2.3.1.
